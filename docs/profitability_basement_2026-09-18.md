## C. Basement design

Four things: instrument, evaluator, protocol, wallet-anchored trader; anything that feeds none of them is deleted.

**Nodes.** VPS (2-core, unreachable): collector and trader, one process at a time: `polymomentum-band-observer.service` (`live --mode paper`, no orders) while halted, `polymomentum-band-canary.service` after promotion, same records. Mac: evaluator and orchestrator (`executable_truth.py --tick` every 15 min under launchd, rsync pull, dead-man alert). PC: off the critical path. Paper mode is the observer (verified: `clob = None` at `pipeline.rs:1489`; wallet preflight and cumulative floor are live-only; `paper_breaker_rearm_reason` never re-arms `kill_switch`); `BAND_OBSERVE_ONLY` only if paper-fill Gamma polling proves costly.

**The ONE evaluator (`scripts/executable_truth.py`).** Table `logs/strategy-research/executable_truth/windows.sqlite3`: one row per (window_start, decision_s in {150,180,210,240}), append-only, build timestamp = accrual cut; columns: Binance basis open/decision, margin, direction, official label, final margin (close(ws+300) - open) with oracle-noise bucket, print summary (writer v2 only), anchor quote, ladder samples, `host`, `git_sha`. Rule = (decision_s, floor_usd in {50 (control),75,100,150}, cap in {0.92,0.94,0.95,0.96,0.97,0.98,0.99}, patience in {0,15,30} s); ask floor 0.80; direction both (up/down is a tripwire, never a rule); no sigma; 336 cells in one pass, 189 registrable (floor >= 75, decision >= 180), the rest diagnostic. Three models per cell: signal-only (ceiling; never gated against BE), print one-look (upper bound, stage 2 only), ladder (truth: trade iff the first sample with t <= patience has `worst_B(t) <= cap`, `vwap_B(t) > 0.80`, fresh books, pair sum 0.90-1.10; entry = `worst`, the FOK limit, never vwap). Per cell: n, W, Wilson lower, mean BE = p + fee*p(1-p), net/USD, halves, up/down, excluded populations' win rate -> `adverse_selected`, oracle ceiling on the final-margin mix (>= BE + 0.5 pp), `tick_fragile` (net at entry + 0.01 <= 0), latency sensitivity (t + 1/2 s), capacity ($25/$100 clear rate). Fee pinned from the 114 `filled` records. Edge-migration series: first second per window with favourite ask > 0.99; a registered cell dies when its 7-day median drifts below its decision second.

**Data plane.** `band_ladder` record (engine, per (window, anchor second)): 1 s samples for 30 s of the momentum-side book at $5/$25/$100 -> `(worst, vwap, shares, depth_limited)`; measured on the Mac 2026-09-18: ~3.8 KB each with a direction (~6-7 KB for the no-direction record a window sampled from mid-start yields, both sides quoted), ~4.3 MB/day at 1,152 records. Band mode ticks on Binance freshness, not chainlink; REST kline backfill on ws gaps in Phase 3. `band_anchor` stays (the race reads it); 270 dropped, 150 added. `host: mac` rows are discovery data only until a >= 3-day Mac/VPS overlap agrees within one tick on >= 95% of shared samples.

**Promotion protocol.** (1) Registration (`deploy/campaigns/<id>.json`) after >= 7 days of ladder rows: registrable cells whose print model clears BE and passes the adverse-selection flag, deduped by accepted-window set; expect N = 3-8; `registered_at` precedes the first accrued window. (2) Accrual: per-cell e-process (`evidence_accrual.EProcess`, PROMOTE_E 20 / FUTILITY_E 0.1) on ladder rows at $25; e-BH at alpha 0.05 with `family_size = N`; one ledger row per (fingerprint, `look_id`). (3) Tripwires, fail closed to `manual_audit`: WR > 0.995 at n >= 100; adverse-selected; halves' lower >= BE - 0.01; directions' lower >= BE - 0.02; WR <= 1 - oracle noise; `tick_fragile`; n >= 100 over >= 14 days; coverage >= 0.9; edge-migration kill. (4) Tier 1 probe: $5 fixed, <= 50 entries, `kelly_q_lo 0`; fill-parity gate over >= 50 entries: fill rate within 10 pp of ladder would-fill, entry worst within 0.01, FOK-kill share <= 20%, `band_price_moved_presubmit` <= 10%. Tier 2: `kelly_q_lo` = the cell's running ladder Wilson lower, cap $25; parity at $25 over >= 150 entries before more. (5) Challengers: paired e-process (`update_signed`) on ladder rows, >= 100 paired windows with trades, K = challengers.

**Sizing and accounting.** stake = f_k * f_lo * equity, f_lo = q_lo - (1 - q_lo)/b; f_k 0.25 during parity, 0.5 after; skip below $5, never clamp up; None above 10% of equity until equity >= $500; cap $25 until $100-budget ladder rows clear. Equity is the RiskBook v2 wallet-anchored book, never pinned. At the candidate cells $5 needs ~$20-33 equity, $25 ~$100-165. Basement at $150: 6-25 trades/day x $5-25 x +1 to +3.5%/USD is $1-10/day; the $100 ladder column decides within 14 days whether that is a business.

**Safety.** All four existing layers stay (kill switch ahead of the feed gate; breaker HOLD across restarts; money floor and streak trip; execution guards: REST arbiter, drift <= 2 ticks, FOK cap, pair coherence, venue incident, wallet-low). Added: consecutive-loss limit 3 for the first 150 entries of any new artifact; money floor 0.25 x v2 equity captured at activation; oracle-disagreement held trip (observed >= expected + 3 pp at n >= 50, or 3 in any 20); breaker consumes Gamma-final outcomes only (tested); v2 refused without `RISK_BOOK_V2_WALLET_EXCLUSIVE=1`; Mac dead-man alert.
